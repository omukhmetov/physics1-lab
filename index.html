Adding and subtracting vectors

Section 1.5 · Example 1.8 · Fig. 1.2

Drag the tips of A and B. Grid squares are 10 m; north is up.

The distance flown is |A| + |B|; the displacement is |A + B|. They are equal only when A and B point the same way.

Components and direction angle

Section 1.6 · Eqs. (1.5)–(1.6) · Caution

A calculator's arctan always returns an angle between −90° and +90°. Check the quadrant from the signs of the components.

About this lab

The Visual Lab accompanies the English-language textbook Physics 1: Mechanics, written for the Physics 1 course at Astana IT University.

Each instrument follows the notation, section numbers and worked examples of the book. The book follows a concept → worked example → quick check → practice structure, and the lab adds a step in the middle: change the inputs and see what the equations do.

The explanations draw on two classic Russian mechanics courses, by N. V. Aleksandrov and A. Ya. Yashkin (1978) and by D. V. Sivukhin (1979), rewritten and extended for an international, English-speaking audience.

Author: Olzhas Mukhmetov, Senior Lecturer. This is an independent course resource, not an official university website.
